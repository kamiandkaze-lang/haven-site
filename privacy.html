Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, how Haven handles information. Haven is a market data and analytics app for Pokémon TCG collectors. It is not yet launched, and this site is currently a simple information page.

What we collect

This website does not use accounts, forms or tracking cookies. If you email us (for example to join the waitlist), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

How we use it

We use your email only to reply to you and, if you asked to join the waitlist, to tell you about Haven's launch. We don't sell your information.

Third parties

Links on this site may take you to other websites, such as eBay, which have their own privacy practices. This site is served by a static hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.

Affiliate links

Haven may earn a commission from eBay purchases made through our links.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any email correspondence at any time by writing to hello@havencoop.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. When the Haven app launches, we will publish a fuller policy covering it.
