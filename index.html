About Haven

Clear data, built for collectors

Haven is a market data and analytics app for Pokémon TCG collectors, made in the US and priced in USD. It brings together prices, past sales and live listings so you can understand a card's market at a glance.

Haven is a research tool. It doesn't provide financial advice, and it never takes payment for listings: when you find a deal, you finish the purchase on eBay.

  • Prices and sales history in one place
  • Watchlists and price alerts for the cards you follow
  • Deal finding with links to buy on eBay
  • Mobile and desktop, in USD
